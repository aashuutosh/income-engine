IncomeEngine is an AI-powered Business Strategy Generator designed specifically for indie hackers and developers. It acts as your AI Business Co-Pilot: just submit your project details, and it instantly generates a comprehensive business plan. 
✨ What it does:
Instant AI Strategy: Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, it analyzes your project to generate an elevator pitch, multiple monetization paths, target demographics, pricing strategies, and competitor analysis in seconds! 
Community Validation: Projects are shared publicly on a community feed where users can upvote, bookmark, and leave real-time feedback to help you refine your ideas. 
Export & Pitch: You can export your generated strategy as a PDF or share it via a public link to pitch to investors, mentors, or clients. 
Admin Controls: A dedicated dashboard for managing user access, moderating comments, and setting global or individual project quotas. 
🛠️ Our Tech Stack:
Backend: Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy 
Frontend: Vanilla JS, HTML5/CSS3, Jinja2 Templates 
AI Engine: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash 
Database: PostgreSQL
